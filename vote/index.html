Questions, Kap?

How the election works.

Why do I need to put my name?

So the election committee can confirm that every vote comes from an official member, and count one vote per Kap. Use the name the group knows you by.

Can I vote for myself?

Yes. An official member may vote for themselves for either the Lead or Co-Lead position, provided they are eligible for the position. Every official member's vote counts, including their vote for themselves.

Can I change my vote?

No. If the same name votes more than once, only the first vote counts. Review your picks before you tap Cast my vote.

Who sees my vote?

Your name and picks go to the election committee's sheet so they can check and count each vote. Only the totals are announced.

What happens if the elected Lead declines or waives the position?

The next eligible official member with the next-highest number of votes may be inducted as Lead, provided that they received at least 7 votes.

Who will assign the officers for the incoming term?

The elected Lead will have the responsibility and discretion to select and assign the officers who will serve alongside him, taking into consideration the needs of the group and the responsibilities of each position.
